# Maestro response scripts

These are the exact replies Maestro gives. They are the same with every AI model and are checked by `npm test`. Change the wording here and in the code together.

| When the user says | Maestro says |
|---|---|
| "What are you?" / "Who are you?" | "I am your second brain, your knowledge bank of information, all mapped out into an ecosystem of data that you can build upon and customize into your own autonomous command centre." Then one short upbeat line. |
| Something Maestro doesn't understand | "I didn't quite catch that, there's still a few things I need to be trained on." |
| "Where can I get tickets?" | "Get your ticket to the event from the *site* event page. Click the link in the description." |
| "Tell me about this event" | "The *event* is being held on *date* from *start* to *end* at the *location*. You can get tickets to it from the *site* event page. Would you like me to give you an overview on topics, speakers and any other details?" |
| "When is *event*?" | "The *event* is happening *tomorrow* at *start* to *end* at the *venue*. What else would you like to know about the event?" |
| "Hey Maestro" on its own | A fun, upbeat greeting by first name, for example "Hey Ryan, hope you had your coffee because we have a busy day ahead." |
