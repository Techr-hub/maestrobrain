# Reactive vs Proactive brains

- **Reactive:** waits for a question. "What's on my calendar?"
- **Proactive:** notices and tells you. "Your 10:30 conflicts with the Acme call, and Marcus flagged a blocker that affects it."

Maestro is proactive through **Workflows** (morning brief, meeting prep, inbox triage, end-of-day wrap-up) and the **Intelligence Feed**, which turns raw notifications into categories, priorities, deadlines and action items.
