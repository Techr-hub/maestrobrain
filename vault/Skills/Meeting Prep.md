# Meeting Prep

1. Find the meeting in `get_calendar`.
2. For each attendee, read their note in `People/` and recent feed items from them.
3. Pull related project notes and open decisions.
4. Output: purpose, what each attendee cares about, 3 talking points, 1 decision to drive.
5. Offer to create a meeting note in `Meetings/` from [[Templates/Meeting Note]].
