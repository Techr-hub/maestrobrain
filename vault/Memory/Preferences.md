# Preferences

## Communication
- Lead with the answer, then the detail. Bullets over paragraphs.
- Stakeholder updates: 4 sentences max, always end with the decision needed.
- Use plain language; avoid jargon with executives.

## Working style
- Deep work 8–10 AM. Protect it: no meetings before 10 if possible.
- Batch Slack and email at 11:30 and 4:00.
- Friday afternoon is for the weekly review ([[Weekly Review]]).

## Brain behaviour
- Flag blockers and conflicts proactively.
- When drafting, write in my voice: direct, warm, short.
- Save meeting outcomes to `Meetings/` and decisions to `Decisions/`.
