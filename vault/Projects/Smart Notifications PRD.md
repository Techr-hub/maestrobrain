# Smart Notifications PRD

## Problem
Users get too many notifications (top NPS complaint: "notification overload"). 38% of churned users disabled notifications in week 1.

## Goals
- Reduce notifications per user per day from 9 to 4
- Increase notification click-through from 3% to 8%

## Proposal
- **Digest mode:** bundle low-priority updates into a daily summary
- **Quiet hours:** user-defined, defaults 9 PM–8 AM (open question from [[Sarah Kim]])
- **Smart priority:** rank by sender importance and deadlines

## Open questions
- Should quiet hours be on by default?
- Do admins get to override for incident alerts?
